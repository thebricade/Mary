OUTSPOKEN DIALOGUE FORMAT
=========================

Each character has one plain text file in this folder (Raoul.txt, Phantom.txt, ...).
Open it in any text editor, change it, save. The next conversation uses the new text. Assign the file to the
NPC prefab's "Dialogue" slot. (An NPC with no file just speaks its simple "Lines" list.)

Mistakes (a node that doesn't exist, a bad beat number...) show up as yellow warnings in the Unity Console
with the line number. Click the warning to select the script.


THE PIECES
----------
# anything         a comment, ignored

=== name           starts a node: one line spoken by a character, plus what Christine can interject
Speaker: text      what the character says (one line in the file). The first node, or the one named "start", begins the talk
|                  inside the text, splits it into BEATS (see below)
>> beats | text -> node
                   something Christine can interject, available during those beats
-> node            where the conversation goes if nobody interjects (-> END finishes it)
~ 3.5              optional: seconds the character waits after finishing, before moving on (default 3)
@ 25               optional: speaking speed for this line, in characters per second (default: the NPC's Talk Speed, 42)


BEATS: THE WHEN OF INTERRUPTING
-------------------------------
Put | in the text to cut it into beats:

    Raoul: Christine! | I have been looking for you | everywhere tonight.
           beat 1       beat 2                        beat 3

While the line is being spoken, the beat changes as the words appear. Nothing about Christine's options is shown
while a character talks. When the player presses Space, the ">>" lines that include the beat at that exact moment
appear. Interrupt early and you get different choices than if you wait.

    >> 1        only during beat 1
    >> 2-3      during beats 2 and 3
    >> 2-end    during beat 2 up to and including the pause after the line
    >> end      only in the pause after the line has finished (the bar is shrinking)
    >> any      always
    >> 1, end   several can be combined with commas

Up to 4 choices are offered at once, in the order written. If no ">>" line fits the moment, Space shows
"(nothing to say)" and the character carries on.


WHAT HAPPENS WHEN CHRISTINE CUTS IN
-----------------------------------
- The character is cut off where they were, with a dash ("I have been looking for--").
- The options for that moment appear. Press 1-4, or use Up/Down (or the mouse wheel) and Space or Enter.
  The conversation waits while the player decides.
- Christine says her chosen line, and the character's cut-off line is shown dimmed above it.
- The conversation continues at the node after "->" on that >> line. If there is no "->", it uses the node's own "->".
- If nobody interjects, the character finishes, pauses, and goes to the node's "->".


EXAMPLE
-------
=== start
Raoul: Christine! | I have been looking for you | everywhere tonight.
>> 1   | Raoul? What are you doing here? -> surprised
>> 2-3 | I have been busy. -> busy
>> end | I am here now. -> warm
-> worried

=== surprised
Raoul: I came to see you, of course.
-> END


SPEED
-----
Each NPC has a Talk Speed in the Inspector (characters per second, default 42). Lower is slower:
    20 = slow and deliberate    42 = normal    70 = fast
A single line can override it with "@ speed" under its text, e.g. a slow, menacing line:

    === threat
    Phantom: You should not have come.
    @ 18

Slower speech also gives more time to interrupt each beat; faster speech makes beats shorter.


TIPS
----
- Quotes around Christine's text are optional.
- Node names are not case sensitive. Use short names with underscores.
- Every node needs a "Speaker: text" line. Christine can also speak in a node (use "Christine: ...").
- Keep NPC lines to one or two sentences. Long ones are fine, but the interjection window is the interesting part.
