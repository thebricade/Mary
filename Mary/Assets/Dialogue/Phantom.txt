# The Phantom. Menacing, patient, quick to take offence.
# Format guide: _Format.txt

=== start
Phantom: You came down here alone. | Either you are very brave, | or very curious.
>> 1   | I came because you called. -> called
>> 2   | I am not afraid of you. -> defiant
>> 3   | Curious, mostly. -> curious
>> end | Why did you send for me? -> called
~ 4
-> silence

=== called
Phantom: Called? | I do not call. | I merely wait, and the willing come.
>> 1-2 | Do not play games with me. -> defiant
>> end | Then I am willing. Teach me. -> teach

=== defiant
Phantom: Brave words, from a girl in the dark. | Be careful. | Courage is only a lack of imagination.
>> 1-2 | Then show me something worth fearing. -> END
>> end | I apologise. -> teach

=== curious
Phantom: Curiosity. | A dangerous talent, | and a useful one.
>> any | Then use it. Teach me. -> teach
-> teach

=== teach
Phantom: Then listen. | A voice is not what you sing with. | It is what you cannot help but sing.
>> 1   | Show me what you mean. -> END
>> 2-3 | I think I understand. -> END
>> end | Teach me, then. -> END

=== silence
Phantom: Nothing to say? | Good. | Silence is the beginning of listening.
-> END
